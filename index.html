About Ana

Hello there! I'm a Game Designer, Play Designer, and a bit of a writer with a creative mind and a love for challenges.

I started my journey as a Game Designer at Skullfish Studios in 2020, and I joined Wildlife a year later. I graduated with a degree in Game Design in 2016, the same year I began working in mobile games as a QA tester and pursuing a Game Designer career.

Currently, I am Senior Game Designer at Wildlife for new games that are to come! In the past I have worked with great projects such as Zooba, Lila's Tale and the Hidden Forest, The Secret Puzzle Society and many small (but very fun!) projects. My current goal is to achieve a leadership position where I can create great games and form new game designers.

In addition to my professional pursuits, I enjoy interacting with people, sharing experiences, and traveling. I have two dogs and two cats who are very dear to me! I'm also a card game enthusiast, very fond of Pokémon and One Piece TCG.
